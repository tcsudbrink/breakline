            Track my day

            Start when you leave the dock. Breakline draws your track, and at the end of the day it asks why you turned where you did and what you caught where. Your catches then tune Where to fish and Find your water to the water you actually catch fish in.

            Phones pause web GPS when the screen locks, so keep Breakline open (it keeps the screen on while tracking) and plugged in.
