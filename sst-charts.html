Questions

Where does the SST come from?

NOAA GOES-19 (hourly), NOAA and NASA polar-orbiting satellites (VIIRS and others) and the NASA JPL MUR cloud-free analysis.

What about clouds?

Clouds block every water-temp satellite. Breakline keeps the newest clear reading for each spot, shows its age, and has cloud-free blends and multi-day composites for cloudy stretches.

How does the free trial work?

Create an account and you get everything for 5 days, no card needed. Then it's $100 a year to keep going. Cancel any time.

Can I navigate with it?

No. Breakline is a fishing planning tool. Always navigate with proper charts and your own electronics.
